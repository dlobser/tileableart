Local Projects Previs — a proposal

We are building a previs tool and we rehearse the room before it exists.

One browser-based space, desktop or VR headset, where designers, lighting, AV, sound and content teams build an experience together, light it with real fixtures, cue the show, fill it with a simulated crowd and render it for clients. All before a single wall goes up.

Behaviour

Real time frames

Walking speed and dwell by age, from visitor studies.
Children move fast and dwell briefly; seniors walk slower and read longer. Films hold watchers for their running time. All parameters can be tuned against the client's own counts.

Social

Groups split and regroup

Families drift apart at exhibits and meet up again.
Groups move at the pace of the slowest member. Members can peel off to another exhibit and rejoin later, which is what actually produces bottlenecks at doorways.

How it’s built

A web app, a Blender bridge and a few proven engines.

Most of the hard parts already exist as mature components. Our own video-driven GI room prototype shows the browser can carry physically based lighting with real-time video light. The work is in joining these pieces into one tool a whole studio can use.

Client

three.js · WebGPU · WebXR

React + R3F interface, imperative render core.
The same split as our R3F prototype: React for UI and interaction, a dedicated lighting engine for GI, LTC area lights, shadows and projection.
